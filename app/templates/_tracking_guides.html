Find your A-Number and receipt number

Two numbers, two different purposes.

Receipt number
The application’s reference number, printed under “Receipt Number.” It has three letters and ten digits, such as ABC1234567890.
A-Number
The person’s immigration identifier. Look beside “Applicant” or “Alien Number.” The example shows A123 456 789. Enter the digits with the optional A prefix.

For family members on one I-589, enter the primary applicant’s A-Number first. Separate the other A-Numbers with commas.

How to pay the Annual Asylum Fee

Follow the USCIS payment portal for your application.

  1. 1

    Open the official payment portal

    Start on the USCIS Annual Asylum Fee page and review its current instructions.

    Go to USCIS payment portal {{ icon('arrow') }}
  2. 2

    Identify your application

    Enter the receipt number and A-Number requested by the portal. Check them against your USCIS notice before continuing.

  3. 3

    Check whether payment is available

    Payment available

    Check the applicant, application, and fee amount, then continue to payment.

    Payment unavailable

    Read the portal’s result and the instructions on your notice before taking another step.

  4. 4

    Enter payment details on the official site

    Use a payment method offered by the portal. If you choose a card, USIRD suggests having a debit card ready. Enter card details only on the official payment page it opens.

  5. 5

    Review, submit, and save confirmation

    Review all details before submitting. Save or print the confirmation and any tracking number displayed. Keep it with your records.

The formal receipt notice can arrive later

USIRD’s experience is that a separate USCIS fee-received notice may arrive about one week to one month after payment. This is an estimate. Keep your payment confirmation while waiting, and check your USCIS account or mail for the formal notice.

Recognize the two notices

Historical, redacted examples. These notices show a past $100 fee. Use the amount and instructions on your own current notice and the official portal.
