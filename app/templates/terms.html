What the service does

We check the public U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) Annual Asylum Fee page, and your USCIS case status through the official USCIS case status service, using the A-Number(s) and I-589 receipt number you give us. We show you the results, re-check daily, and email you when a result changes.

What it is not

  • We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for USCIS, EOIR, DHS, DOJ or any government agency.
  • We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. Talk to an immigration attorney or accredited representative about your case.
  • We never collect fees for USCIS. Pay any fee only on the official USCIS website.

Accuracy

Results come from the public USCIS page at the time of the check and can be delayed, incomplete or wrong, for example if USCIS changes its site or is unavailable. Always confirm on the official site before acting. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for missed fees, deadlines or other harm from relying on the service.

Your responsibilities

Only add cases that are yours, or that you are authorized to track (for example your family or your clients with their permission). Do not use the service to look up other people's cases. We may close accounts that misuse it.
