Questions

Where does the information come from?

The asylum fee status comes from the official USCIS Annual Asylum Fee page. The case status comes from the official USCIS case status service. We check them for you and show the result.

Do you pay the fee for me?

No. We tell you when a fee is due. You pay only on the official USCIS website.

Do I need my USCIS account password?

No. We only need your A-Number and I-589 receipt number.

Is this legal advice?

No. USIRD Case Tracker is an information service. For advice about your case, talk to an immigration attorney or accredited representative.

How do I cancel?

Cancel your Square subscription anytime. You can also delete your account and all case data from your dashboard.
