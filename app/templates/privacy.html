Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address, a hashed password, and any profile photo you choose to upload. You can remove your photo from your account.
  • We store the primary applicant’s name, date of birth, contact phone and contact email encrypted on our server.
  • For each case: the A-Number(s), I-589 receipt number, an optional name you choose, and the check results.
  • Payment status and account references from Square. Card numbers and security codes are handled by Square and never reach us. We send Square your account email and the name you enter for billing.

If you use the ICE lookup helper, you can save a country of birth for each person. We store it encrypted and use it to prepare the lookup details you enter directly on ICE’s website. If you request staff checks, authorized staff use those details to search the locator and record the result in your account. For EOIR staff checks, staff use the primary person’s A-Number and the nationality you supply.

How we use it

Only to run the checks you asked for, show you results, and email you about your cases and your account. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who sees it

Your A-Numbers and receipt numbers are sent to the USCIS website to perform each check. They are stored encrypted on our server and shown back to you only partly (last digits). A small number of USIRD staff can access the system for support and security.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting a case or your account removes the numbers and history right away; encrypted backups roll off within 30 days.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, case numbers are encrypted at rest, and passwords are stored with a one-way hash. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reply to any email from us.
